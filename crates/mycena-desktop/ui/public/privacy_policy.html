Mycena Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Mycena is a decentralized mesh messenger. There is no central account server. This policy describes what data exists, where it goes, and what we (the app publisher) do and do not control.

Summary

  • We do not operate a user database or sell analytics.
  • Your messages are stored on your device and may be copied by other users’ devices on the mesh.
  • Direct chats can be end-to-end encrypted when keys are exchanged; other modes differ (see below).
  • Using the Internet relay exposes your IP address to the relay operator for connection setup.
  • We are pseudonymous, not anonymous: peers and observers may learn who you talk to, when, and how much traffic you send.

Data controller

The Mycena project publishes the Mycena app. For Play Store distribution, the Mycena project is the data controller for app listing and support contact below. Mesh traffic is peer-to-peer; we do not host message content on our servers.

What is stored on your device

  • Cryptographic identity keys (account and transport keys), encrypted where the OS allows.
  • Messages (chat, mail, bulletin) in a local database, subject to expiry and your settings.
  • Contacts, groups, and encryption key material you create or accept.
  • MeshCoin wallet data (experimental utility credits — not bank money).
  • Mini-app data in per-app local storage when you install mini-apps.
  • Network metadata such as peer trust scores and encounter logs (used for routing and custody).

You can delete local data with Settings → Panic wipe. That removes keys and databases on this device only. Copies already stored on other users’ devices or forwarded in the mesh are not deleted by us — we have no central copy to erase.

What leaves your device

When you use the mesh, other participants and infrastructure may observe:

  • Peer identifiers (libp2p PeerId and related public keys).
  • Display name you choose (if set).
  • Encryption public keys exchanged for direct messaging.
  • Message payloads — encrypted for direct chat when E2E is active; bulletin/mail formats depend on scope and settings.
  • Metadata: timing, message size, subscribed bulletin scopes, sync activity, and MeshCoin gossip if enabled (not message plaintext for E2E chat).
  • Custody (store-and-forward) envelopes — encrypted content plus routing fields (destination peer, hop count, timing) when messages are held for later delivery.

Internet relay

When you use the default Internet path, connections may pass through a public relay (e.g. community or project-operated libp2p relay). The relay operator can typically see:

  • Your IP address and the fact that you use libp2p/Mycena.
  • Circuit endpoints (which peer IDs connect through the relay).
  • Timing and volume of traffic (not E2E message plaintext when encryption is used correctly).

Policy intent: relay operators should not retain IP logs beyond what is technically required for abuse prevention. You can limit relay use via connectivity settings (mesh-only / LAN).

What we (the Mycena project) collect

  • No in-app analytics or advertising identifiers in the open-source client.
  • No central registration or account database operated by us.
  • Support contact if you email us (see Contact).
  • Play Store may collect install and crash data per Google’s policies — see Google Play data safety.

End-to-end encryption — honest limits

  • Direct chat: Can be E2E encrypted after keys are exchanged. The app blocks sending without a peer encryption key (fail-closed).
  • Groups: Use a shared group key from an invite — anyone with the invite can read history.
  • Bulletin boards: Scoped channels; treat as public-to-scope unless stated otherwise.
  • Metadata: E2E does not hide that you communicate, with whom (peer IDs), when, or how much data you send.

MeshCoin (experimental)

MeshCoin (MXC) is an experimental local utility credit, not legal tender. Balances and transactions may be gossiped to peers on the coin scope. Offline payments via vouchers may remain unsettled until online merge; double-spend across network partitions is detected retroactively, not prevented upfront. Do not treat MXC as savings.

Mini-apps

Third-party mini-apps run in a sandboxed WebView with permission-gated APIs (messaging, payments, storage, etc.). Store listings require a developer signature; sideload installs require a valid bundle signature file. Only install apps you trust. A malicious app with granted permissions can abuse the bridge within those permissions.

Children

Mycena is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights (GDPR / similar)

Because we do not operate a central account system:

  • Access / portability: Data is on your device; export features vary by release.
  • Erasure: Panic wipe erases local data. We cannot erase mesh copies on other devices.
  • Objection / restriction: You can stop the node, use mesh-only mode, or uninstall.

EU users: contact us if you operate a relay or enterprise rollout that processes IP addresses at scale.

Security and high-risk users

Not safe against: nation-state traffic analysis, global Sybil attacks, or device seizure without panic wipe. Do not rely on Mycena alone for life-threatening or high-risk activism. More detail: security-for-users, KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.

Changes

We update this policy when data practices change. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date and, where required, in-app notice.
